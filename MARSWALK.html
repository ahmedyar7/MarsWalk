THE PROBLEM
In 2009, NASA’s Spirit rover drove into soft Martian soil and never moved again.
Soft sand and firm soil look nearly identical in a photograph. An astronaut in a pressurised suit — restricted visor, carrying instruments — has even less information than that rover did.
Mars Trek and the USGS-NASA geologic maps are built for regional and global analysis. Nothing works at the scale of a single excursion: the ground a crew member actually crosses on one walk.

PRECISION
81% of false positives are bedrock
The model does not miss obstacles. It over-calls them, almost entirely on solid rock. For a safety tool that is the correct direction to fail: it routes a crew around bedrock they could have crossed, rather than into a rock they would trip over.
AI4Mars frames carry no rover coordinates. Elevation, slope and the route are real geospatial data; the terrain classification and every reported metric are measured. Imaging station placement is illustrative.
